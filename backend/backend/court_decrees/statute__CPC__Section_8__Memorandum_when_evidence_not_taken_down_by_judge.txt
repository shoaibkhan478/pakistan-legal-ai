CPC Section 8: Memorandum when evidence not taken down by judge

_
Where the
evidence is not taken  down in writing by the Judge, he shall be bound, as the
examination of each witness proceeds, to make a memorandum of the substance
of what each witness deposes, and such memorandum shall be written and signed
by the Judge and shall form part of the record.
