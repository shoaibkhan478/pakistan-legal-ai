CPC Section 91: Application by purchaser to set aside sale on ground of

judgment debtor having no saleable interest.
_
The purchaser at any such
sale in execution of a decree may apply to the Court to set aside the sale, on the
ground that the judgment-debtor had no saleable interest in the property sold.
