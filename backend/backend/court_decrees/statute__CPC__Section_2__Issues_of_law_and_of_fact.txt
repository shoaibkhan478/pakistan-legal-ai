CPC Section 2: Issues of law and of fact

_
Where issues both of law and of fact arise in
the same suit, and the Court is of opinion that the case or any part thereof may be
disposed of on the issues of law only, it shall try those issues first, and for that
purpose may, if it thinks fit, postpone the settlement of the issues of fact until after
the issues of law have been determined.
