CPC Section 15: Where service may be on male member of defendant`s family

_
Where in any suit the defendant cannot be found and has no agent
empowered to accept service of the summons on his behalf, service may be
made on any adult male member of the family of the defendant who is residing
with him.
Explanation.
_
A servant is not a member of the family within the meaning of
this rule.
