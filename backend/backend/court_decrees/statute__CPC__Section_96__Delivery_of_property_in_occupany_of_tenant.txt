CPC Section 96: Delivery of property in occupany of tenant

_
Where the property sold
is in the occupancy of a tenant or other person entitled to occupy the same and a
certificate in respect thereof has been granted under rule 94, the Court shall, on
the application of the purchaser, order delivery to be made by affixing a copy
of the certificate of sale in some conspicuous place on the property, and
proclaiming to the occupant by beat of drum or other customary mode, at some
convenient place, that the interest of the judgment-debtor has been transferred to
the purchaser.
