CPC Section 15: Refusal to allow applicant to sue as pauper to bar

subsequent application of like nature.
_
An order refusing to allow the
applicant to sue as a pauper shall be a bar to any subsequent application of the
like nature by him in respect of the same right to sue; but the applicant shall be
at liberty to institute a suit in the ordinary manner in respect of such right, provided
that he first pays the costs (if any) incurred by the
1
[Provincial Government] and
by the opposite party in opposing his application for leave to sue as a pauper.
