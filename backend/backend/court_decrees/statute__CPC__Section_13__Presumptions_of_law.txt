CPC Section 13: Presumptions of law

_
Neither party need in any pleading allege any
matter of fact which the law presumes in his favour or as to which the burden of
proof lies upon the other side unless the same has first been specifically denied
(e.g., consideration for a bill of exchange where the plaintiff sues only on the bill
and not for the consideration as a substantive ground of claim).
