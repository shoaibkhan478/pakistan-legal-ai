CPC Section 2: Contents of application

_
Every application for permission to sue as a
pauper shall contain the  particulars required in regard to plaints in suits a
schedule of any moveable or immoveable property belonging to the applicant,
with the estimated value thereof, shall be annexed thereto; and it shall be signed
and verified in the manner prescribed for the signing and verification of pleadings.
