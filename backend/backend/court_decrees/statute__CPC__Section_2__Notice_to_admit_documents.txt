CPC Section 2: Notice to admit documents

_
Either party may call upon the other party
to admit any document, saving all just exceptions; and in case of refusal or
neglect to admit, after such notice, the costs of proving any such document shall
be paid by the party so neglecting or refusing, whatever the result of the suit may
be, unless the Court otherwise directs; and no costs of proving any document
shall be allowed unless such notice is given, except where the omission to give
the notice is, in the opinion of the Court, a saving of expense.
