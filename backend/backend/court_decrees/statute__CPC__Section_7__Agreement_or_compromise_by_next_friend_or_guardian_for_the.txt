CPC Section 7: Agreement or compromise by next friend or guardian for the

suit.
_
(1) No next friend or guardian for the suit shall, without the leave of the
Court, expressly recorded in the proceedings, enter into any agreement or
compromise on behalf of a minor with reference to the suit in which he acts as
next friend or guardian.
(2) Any such agreement or compromise entered into without the leave of
the Court so recorded shall be voidable against all parties other than the minor.
