CPC Section 8: Court may order any document to be impounded

_
Notwithstanding
anything contained in rule  5 or rule 7 of this Order or in rule 17 of Order VII, the
Court may, if it sees sufficient cause, direct any document or book produced
before it in any suit to be impounded and kept in the custody of an officer of the
Court, for such period and subject to such conditions as the Court thinks fit.
