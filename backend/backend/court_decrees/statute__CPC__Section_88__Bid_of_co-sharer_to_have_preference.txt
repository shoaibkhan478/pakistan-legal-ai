CPC Section 88: Bid of co-sharer to have preference

_
Where the property sold is
a share of undivided immoveable property and two or more persons, of whom
one is a co-sharer, respectively bid the same sum for such property or for any lot,
the bid shall be deemed to be the bid of the co-sharer.
