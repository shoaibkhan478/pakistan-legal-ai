CPC Section 2: Procedure if parties fail to appear on day fixed

_
Where, on any day
to which the hearing of the suit is adjourned, the parties or any of them fail to
appear, the Court may proceed to dispose of the suit in one of the modes directed
in that behalf by Order IX or make such other order as it thinks fit.
