CPC Section 10: Procedure in case of non-attendance of one or more of several

plaintiffs.
_
Where there are more plaintiffs than one, and one or more of them
appear, and the others do not appear, the Court may, at the instance of the
plaintiff or plaintiffs appearing, permit the suit to proceed in the same way as if all
the plaintiffs had appeared, or make such order as it thinks fit.
