CPC Section 1: Power to order any point to be proved by affidavit

_
Any Court may
at any time for sufficient reason order that any particular fact or facts may be
proved by affidavit, or that the affidavit of any witness may be read at the hearing,
on such conditions as the Court thinks reasonable:
Provided that where it appears to the Court that either party bona fide
desires the production of a witness for cross-examination, and that such witness
can be produced, and order shall not be made authorizing the evidence of such
witness to be given by affidavit.
