CPC Section 6: Attendance of person able to answer questions relating to suit

against Government.
_
The Court may also, in any case in which the
9
[Government pleader] is not accompanied by any person on the part of
7
[the
Government], who may be able to answer any material questions relating to the
suit, direct the attendance of such a person.
