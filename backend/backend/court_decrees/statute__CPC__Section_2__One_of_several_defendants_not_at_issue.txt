CPC Section 2: One of several defendants not at issue

_
Where there are more
defendants than one, and any one of the defendants is not at issue with the
plaintiff on any question of law or of fact, the Court may at once pronounce
judgment for or against such defendant and the suit shall proceed only against
the other defendants.
