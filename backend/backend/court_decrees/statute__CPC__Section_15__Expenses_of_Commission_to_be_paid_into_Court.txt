CPC Section 15: Expenses of Commission to be paid into Court

_
Before issuing any
commission under this Order, the Court may order such sum  (if any) as it
thinks reasonable for the expenses of the commission to be, within a time to
be fixed, paid into Court by the party at whose instance or for whose benefit the
commission is issued.
