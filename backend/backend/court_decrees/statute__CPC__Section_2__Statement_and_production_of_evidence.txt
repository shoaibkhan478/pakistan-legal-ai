CPC Section 2: Statement and production of evidence

_
(1) On the day fixed for the
hearing of the suit or on any other day to which the hearing is adjourned, the party
having the right to begin shall state his case and produce his evidence in support
of the issues which he is bound to prove.
1

(2) The other party shall then state his case and produce his evidence (if
any) and may then address the Court generally on the whole case.
(3) The party beginning may then reply generally on the whole case.
