CPC Section 1: Fixation of Intermediate dates

_
(1) After the close of the pleadings, the
Court shall fix
_
(a) a day by which parties shall apply for orders of the Court
with regard to any of the following matters, namely,
__
Pleadings further and better particulars, admissions,
discoveries, inspection of documents or movable or
immovable property and the mode by which particular facts
may be proved.
(b) another day by which parties may reply such applications; and
(c) a third day on which, unless the hearing is adjourned, the
applications shall be disposed of.
2. Applications regarding pleadings, etc., their replies and disposal.
_
No opportunity shall be given to any party for making any such application as
aforesaid or for submitting a reply thereto after the expiry of the day fixed for
that purpose, unless the time is enlarged under the provisions of this Code; but
nothing herein shall affect the right of the parties to make such applications
before the closing of the pleadings.]
