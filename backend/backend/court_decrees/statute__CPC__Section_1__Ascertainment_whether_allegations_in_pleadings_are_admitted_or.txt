CPC Section 1: Ascertainment whether allegations in pleadings are admitted or

denied.
_
At the first hearing of the suit the Court shall ascertain from each party
or his pleader whether he admits or denies such allegations of fact as are made
in the plaint or written statement (if any) of the opposite party, and as are not
expressly or by necessary implication admitted or denied by the party against
whom they are made. The Court shall record such admissions and denials.
