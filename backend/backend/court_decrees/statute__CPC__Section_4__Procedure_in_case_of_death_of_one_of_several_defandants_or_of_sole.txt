CPC Section 4: Procedure in case of death of one of several defandants or of sole

defandant.
_
(1) Where one of two or more defendants dies and the right to
sue does not survive against the surviving defendant or defendants alone
1
[,or
on receipt of an intimation of the death of such defendant from the person
nominated by him for that purpose under rule 13, Order VIII], or a sole
defendant or sole surviving defendant dies and the right to sue survives, the Court
,on an application made in that behalf, shall cause the legal representative of the
deceased defendant to be made a party and shall proceed with the suit.
(2) Any person so made a party may make any defence appropriate
to his character as legal representative of the deceased defendant.
