CPC Section 3: The highest bidder shall be declared to be the purchaser of any lot, provided

always that he is legally qualified to bid, and provided that it shall be in the
discretion of the Court or officer holding the sale to decline acceptance of the
highest bid when the price offered appears so clearly inadequate as to make it
advisable to do so.
