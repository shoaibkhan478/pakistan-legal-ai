CPC Section 49: Attachment of partnership property

_
(1) Save as otherwise provided
by this rule, property  belonging to a partnership shall not be attached or sold in
execution of a decree other than a decree passed against the firm or against the
partners in the firm as such.
(2) The Court may, on the application of the holder of a decree against
a partner make an order charging the interest of such partner in the
partnership property and profits with payment of the amount due under the
decree, and may, by the same or a subsequent order, appoint a receiver of the
share of such partner in the profits  (whether already declared or accruing)
and of any other money which may be coming to him in respect of the
partnership, and direct accounts and inquiries and make an order for the sale of
such interest or other orders as might have been directed or made if a charge
had been made in favour of the decree-holder by such partner, or as the
circumstances of the case may require.
(3) The other partner or partners shall be at liberty at any time to redeem
the interest charged, or, in the case of a sale being directed, to purchase the same.
(4) Every application for an order under sub-rule  (2) shall be served on
the judgment-debtor and on his partners or such of them as are within
9
[Pakistan].
(5) Every application made by any partner of the judgment-debtor
under sub-rule  (3) shall be served on the decree-holder and on the
judgment-debtor, and on such of the other partners as do not join in the
application and as are within
10
[Pakistan].
