CPC Section 19: Examination of serving officer

_
Where a summons is returned under
rule 17, the Court shall, if the return under that rule has not been verified by the
affidavit of the serving officer, and may, if it has been so verified, examine the
serving officer on oath, or cause him to be so examined by another Court,
touching his proceedings, and may make such further inquiry in the matter as it
thinks fit; and shall either declare that the summons has been duly served or order
such service as it thinks fit.
