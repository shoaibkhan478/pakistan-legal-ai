CPC Section 6: Power to refer to High Court questions as jursidiction in small

causes.– (1) Where at any time before judgment a Court in which a suit has
been instituted doubts whether the suit is cognizable by a Court of Small Causes
or is not so cognizable, it may submit the record to the High Court with a
statement of its reasons for the doubt as to the nature of the suit.
(2) On receiving the record and statement, the High Court may order
the Court either to proceed with the suit or to return the plaint for presentation to
such other Court as it may in its order declare to be competent to take cognizance
of the suit.
