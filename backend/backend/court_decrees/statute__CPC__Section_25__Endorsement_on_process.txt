CPC Section 25: Endorsement on process

_
(1) The officer entrusted with the
execution of the process shall endorse thereon the day on, and the manner in,
which it was executed, and, if the latest day specified in the process for the
return thereof has been exceeded, the reason of the delay, or, if it was not
executed, the reason why it was not executed, and shall return the process with
such endorsement to the Court.
(2) Where the endorsement is to the effect that such officer is unable
to execute the process, the Court shall examine him touching his alleged inability,
and may, if it thinks fit, summon and examine witnesses as to such inability, and
shall record the result.
