CPC Section 27: Service on civil public officer or on servant of railway company or

local authority.
_
Where the defendant is a public officer (not belonging to
2
[the
armed forces of Pakistan]
3
[* * *]), or is the servant of a railway company or
local authority, the Court may, if it appears to it that the summons may be most
conveniently so served, send it for service on the defendant to the head of the
office in which he is employed, together with a copy to be retained by the
defendant.
