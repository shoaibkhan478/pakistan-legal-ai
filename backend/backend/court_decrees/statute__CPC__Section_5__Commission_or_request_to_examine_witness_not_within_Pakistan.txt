CPC Section 5: Commission or request to examine witness not within Pakistan

_
Where any Court to which application is made for the issue of commission for the
examination of a person residing at any place not within Pakistan is satisfied that
the evidence of such person is necessary, the Court may issue such commission
or a letter of request.
