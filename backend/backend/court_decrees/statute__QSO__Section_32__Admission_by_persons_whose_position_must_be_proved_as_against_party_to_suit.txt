QSO Section 32: Admission by persons whose position must be proved as against party to suit

Statements made by persons, whose position or liability it is necessary to prove as against any party to the suit,
are admissions, if such statements would be relevant as against such persons in relation to such position or liability
in a suit brought by or against them, and if they are made whilst the person making them occupies such position
or is subject to such liability.
Illustrations
A undertakes to collect rents for B.

33. Admission by persons expressly referred to by party to suit.— Statements made by persons
to whom a party to the suit has expressly referred for information in reference to matter in dispute are admissions.
Illustrations
The question is, whether a horse sold by A to B is sound.
A says to B: “Go and ask C: C knows all about it.” C’s statement is an admission.
