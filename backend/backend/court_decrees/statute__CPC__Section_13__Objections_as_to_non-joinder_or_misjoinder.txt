CPC Section 13: Objections as to non-joinder or misjoinder

_
All objections on the
ground of non-joinder or misjoinder of parties shall be taken at the earliest possible
opportunity and, in all cases where issues are settled, at or before such
settlement, unless the ground of objection has subsequently arisen, and any such
objection not so taken shall be deemed to have been waived.
