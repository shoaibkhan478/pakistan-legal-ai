CPC Section 14: Service on agent in charge in suits for immoveable property

_
Where in a suit to obtain relief respecting, or compensation for wrong to,
immoveable property, service cannot be made on the defendant in person, and
the defendant has no agent empowered to accept the service, it may be made on
any agent of the defendant in charge of the property.
