CPC Section 55: Removal of attachment after satisfaction of decree

_
Where
_
(a) the amount decreed with costs and all charges and expenses
resulting from the attachment of any property are paid into Court, or
(b) satisfaction of the decree is otherwise made through the Court or
certified to the Court, or (c) the decree is set aside or reversed, the
attachment shall be deemed to be withdrawn, and, in the case of
immovable property, the withdrawal shall, if the judgment-debtor so
desires, be proclaimed at his expense, and a copy of the
proclamation shall be affixed in the manner prescribed by the last
preceding rule.
