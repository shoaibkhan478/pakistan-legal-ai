CPC Section 9: Dispaupering

_
The Court may, on the application of the defendant, or
of the Government pleader, of which seven day's clear notice in writing has been
given to the plaintiff, order the plaintiff to be dispaupered-
(a) if he is guilty of vexatious or improper conduct in the course of the suit;
(b) if it appears that his means are such that he ought not to continue to sue
as a pauper; or
(c) if he has entered into any agreement with reference to the
subject-matter of the suit under which any other person has obtained an interest in
such subject-matter.
