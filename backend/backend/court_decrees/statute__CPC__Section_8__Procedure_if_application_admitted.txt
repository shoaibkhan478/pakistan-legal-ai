CPC Section 8: Procedure if application admitted

_
Where the application is granted, it
shall be numbered and registered, and shall be deemed the plaint in the suit, and
the suit shall proceed in all other respects as a suit instituted in the ordinary
manner, except that plaintiff shall not be liable to pay [any court-fee  (other
than fee payable for service of process) in respect of any petition, appointment of
a pleader or other proceeding connected with the suit.
