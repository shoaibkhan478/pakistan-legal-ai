CPC Section 2: Oral examination of party or companion of party

_
At the first hearing
of the suit, or at any subsequent hearing, any party appearing in person or
present in Court, or any person able to answer any material questions relating to
the suit by whom such party or his pleader is accompanied,
1
[shall] be examined
orally by the Court; and the Court may, if it thinks fit, put in the course of
such examination questions suggested by either party.
