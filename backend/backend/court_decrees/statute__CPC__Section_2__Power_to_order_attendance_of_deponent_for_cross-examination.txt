CPC Section 2: Power to order attendance of deponent for cross-examination

_
(1) Upon any application evidence may be given by affidavit, but the Court
may, at the instance of either party, order the attendance for cross-examination
of the deponent.
(2) Such attendance shall be in Court, unless the deponent is
exempted from personal appearance in Court, or the Court otherwise directs.
