CPC Section 4: Judgments of Small Cause Courts

_
(1) Judgments of a Court of Small
Causes need not contain more than the points for determination and the decision
thereon.
(2) Judgments of other Courts.
_
Judgments of other Courts shall
contain a concise statement of the case, the points for determination, the decision
thereon, and the reasons for such decision.
