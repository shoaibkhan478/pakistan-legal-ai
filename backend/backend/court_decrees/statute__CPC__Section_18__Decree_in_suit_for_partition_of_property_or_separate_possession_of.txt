CPC Section 18: Decree in suit for partition of property or separate possession of

a share therein.
_
Where the Court passes a decree for the partition of property
or for the separate possession of a share therein, then,
_
(1) if and in so far as the decree relates to an estate assessed
to the payment of revenue to the Government, the decree
shall declare the rights of the several parties interested in
the property, but shall direct such partition or separation to be
made by the Collector, or any gazetted subordinate of the
Collector deputed by him in this behalf, in accordance with
such declaration and with the provisions of section 54;
(2) if and in so far as such decree relates to any other
immoveable property or to moveable property, the Court may,
if the partition or separation cannot be conveniently made
without further inquiry, pass a preliminary decree declaring the
rights of the several parties interested in the property and
giving such further directions as may be required.
