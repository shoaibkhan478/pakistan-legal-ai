CPC Section 1: Right to begin

_
The plaintiff has the right to begin unless the defendant
admits the facts alleged by the plaintiff and contends that either in point of law
or on some additional facts alleged by the defendant the plaintiff is not entitled
to any part of the relief which he seeks, in which case the defendant has the
right to begin.
