CPC Section 7: The full amount of the purchase-money shall be paid by the purchaser before

the Court closes on the fifteenth day after the sale of the property, exclusive of
such day, or if the fifteenth day be a Sunday or other holiday, then on the first
office day after the fifteenth day.
