CPC Section 1: Discovery by interrogatories

_
In any suit the plaintiff or defendant by leave
of the Court may deliver interrogatories in writing for the examination of the
opposite parties or any one or more of such parties, and such interrogatories when
delivered shall have a note at the foot thereof stating which of such
interrogatories each of such persons is required to answer: Provided that no
party shall deliver more than one set of interrogatories to the same party without
an order for that purpose: Provided also that interrogatories which do not relate to
any matters in question in the suit shall be deemed irrelevant, notwithstanding that
they might be admissible on the oral cross-examination of a witness.
