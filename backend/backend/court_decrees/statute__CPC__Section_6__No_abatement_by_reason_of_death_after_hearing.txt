CPC Section 6: No abatement by reason of death after hearing

_
Notwithstanding
anything contained in the foregoing rules, whether the cause of action survives or
not, there shall be no abatement by reason of the death of either party between
the conclusion of the hearing and the pronouncing of the judgment, but judgment
may in such case be pronounced notwithstanding the death and shall have the
same force and effect as if it had been pronounced before the death took place.
