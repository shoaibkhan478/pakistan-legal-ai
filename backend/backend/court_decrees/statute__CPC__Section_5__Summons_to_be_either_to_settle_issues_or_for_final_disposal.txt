CPC Section 5: Summons to be either to settle issues or for final disposal

_
The
Court shall determine, at the time of issuing the summons, whether it shall be
for the settlement of issues only, or for the final disposal of the suit; and the
summons shall contain a direction accordingly:
Provided that, in every suit heard by a Court of small Causes, the
summons shall be for the final disposal of the suit.
