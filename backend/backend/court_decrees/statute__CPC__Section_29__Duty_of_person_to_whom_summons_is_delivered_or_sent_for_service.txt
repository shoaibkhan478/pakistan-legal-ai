CPC Section 29: Duty of person to whom summons is delivered or sent for service

_
(1)Where a summons is delivered or sent to any person for service under rule
24, rule 27 or rule 28, such person shall be bound to serve it, if possible, and to
return it under his signature, with the written acknowledgement of the defendant,
and such signature shall be deemed to be evidence of service.
