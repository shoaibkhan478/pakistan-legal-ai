CPC Section 28: Order of Court which passed decree or of appellate Court to be

binding upon Court applied to.
_
Any order of the Court by which the
decree was passed, or of such Court of appeal as aforesaid, in relation to the
execution of such decree, shall be binding upon the Court to which the decree
was sent for execution.
