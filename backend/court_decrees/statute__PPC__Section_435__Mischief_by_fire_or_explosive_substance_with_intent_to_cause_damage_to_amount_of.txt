PPC Section 435: Mischief by fire or explosive substance with intent to cause damage to amount of one

hundred or (in case of agricultural produce) ten rupees. Whoever commits mischief by fire or any
explosive substance, intending to cause, or knowing it to be likely that he will thereby cause, damage
to any property to the amount of one hundred rupees or upwards
