CPC Section 8: On issue of summons for final disposal, defendant to be

directed to produce his witnesses.
_
Where the summons is for the final
disposal of the suit, it shall also direct the defendant to produce, on the day fixed
for his appearance, all witnesses upon whose evidence he intends to rely in
support of his case.
