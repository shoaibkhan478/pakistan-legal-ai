QSO Section 153: Former statements of witness may be proved to corroborate later testimony

as to same fact. — In order to corroborate the testimony of a witness, any former statement made by
such  witness  relating  to  the  same  fact  at  or  about  the  time  when  the  fact  took  place,  or  before  any
authority legally competent to investigate the facts, may be proved.
