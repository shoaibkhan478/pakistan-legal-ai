PPC Section 248: Altering appearance of coin with intent that it shall pass as coin of different description

Whoever  performs  on  any  coin  any  operation  which  alters  the  appearance  of  that  coin, with the
intention  that  the said coin  shall  pass  as  a  coin  of  a  different  description,  shall  be  punished with
imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years, and shall also be liable
to fine.
