CPC Section 5: Mode of transfer

_
Where the Court to which a decree is to be sent for
execution is situate within the same district as the Court which passed such
decree, such Court shall send the same directly to the former Courts. But, where
the Court to which the decree is to be sent for execution is situate in a
different district, the Court which passed it shall send it to the District Court of the
district in which the decree is to be executed.
