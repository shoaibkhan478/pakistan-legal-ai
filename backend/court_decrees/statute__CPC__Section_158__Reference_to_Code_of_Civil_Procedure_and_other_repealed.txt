CPC Section 158: Reference to Code of Civil Procedure and other repealed

enactments.– In every enactment or notification passed or issued before the
commencement of this Code in which reference is made to or to any Chapter or
section of Act VIII of 1859 or any Code of Civil Procedure or any Act amending
the same or any other enactment hereby repealed, such reference shall, so far as
may be practicable, be taken to be made to this Code or to its corresponding Part,
Order, section or rule.
