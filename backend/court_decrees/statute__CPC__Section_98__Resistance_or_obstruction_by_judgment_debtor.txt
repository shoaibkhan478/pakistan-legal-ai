CPC Section 98: Resistance or obstruction by judgment debtor

_
Where the
Court is satisfied that the resistance or obstruction was occasioned without any
just cause by the judgment-debtor or by some other person at his instigation, it
shall direct that the applicant be put into possession of the property, and where
the applicant is still resisted or obstructed in obtaining possession, the Court may
also, at the instance of the applicant, order the judgment-debtor, or any person
acting at his instigation, to be detained in
1
[* *] prison for a term which may extend
to thirty days.
