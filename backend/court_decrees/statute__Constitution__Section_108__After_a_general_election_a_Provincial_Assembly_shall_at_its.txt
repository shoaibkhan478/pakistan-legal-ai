Constitution Section 108: After a general election, a Provincial Assembly shall, at its

first meeting and to the exclusion of any other business, elect
from amongst its members a Speaker and a Deputy Speaker and,
so often as the office of Speaker or Deputy Speaker becomes
vacant, the Assembly shall elect another member as Speaker or,
as the case may be, Deputy Speaker.
