PPC Section 355: Assault  or  criminal  force  with  intent  to  dishonour  person,  otherwise  than  on  grave

provocation. Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any person, intending thereby to dishonour that person,
otherwise than on grave and sudden provocation given by that person, shall be punished with imprisonment of
either description for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.
