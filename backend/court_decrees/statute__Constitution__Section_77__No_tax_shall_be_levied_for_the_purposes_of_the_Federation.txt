Financial Procedure
78.   (1)    All revenues received by the Federal Government, all
loans raised by that Government, and all moneys received by it in
repayment of any loan, shall form part of a consolidated fund, to
be known as the Federal Consolidated Fund.
(2)    All other moneys–
(a)    received by or on behalf of the Federal
Government; or
(b)    received by or deposited with
2

Federation;
