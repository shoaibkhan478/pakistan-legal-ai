CPC Section 14: Production of document on which plaintiff sues

_
(1) Where a
plaintiff sues upon a document in his possession or power, he shall produce it
in Court when the plaint is presented, and shall at the same time deliver the
document or a copy thereof to be filed with the plaint.
(2) List of other documents.
_
Where he relies on any other
documents (whether in his possession or power or not) as evidence in support of
his claim, he shall enter such documents in a list to be added or annexed to the
plaint.
