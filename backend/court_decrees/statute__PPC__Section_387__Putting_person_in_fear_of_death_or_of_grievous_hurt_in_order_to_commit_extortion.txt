PPC Section 387: Putting  person  in  fear  of  death  or  of  grievous  hurt,  in  order  to  commit  extortion

Whoever, in order to the committing of extortion puts or attempts to put any person in fear of death  or
of grievous hurt to that person or to any other, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description
for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.
