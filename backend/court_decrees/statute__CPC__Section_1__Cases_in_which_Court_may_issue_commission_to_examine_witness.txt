CPC Section 1: Cases in which Court may issue commission to examine witness

_
Any court may in any suit issue a commission for the examination on
interrogatories or otherwise of any person resident within the local limits of its
jurisdiction who is exempted under this Code from attending the Court or who is
from sickness or infirmity unable to attend it.
