CPC Section 7: Recording of admitted and return of rejected documents

_
(1)
Every document which has been admitted in evidence, or a copy thereof where a
copy has been substituted for the original under rule 5, shall form part of the
record of the suit.
(2) Documents not admitted in evidence shall not form part of the record
and shall be returned to the persons respectively producing them.
