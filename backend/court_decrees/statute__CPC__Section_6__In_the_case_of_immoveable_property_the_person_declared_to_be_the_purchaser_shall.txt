CPC Section 6: In the case of immoveable property, the person declared to be the purchaser shall

pay immediately after such declaraticn a deposit 25 per cent on the amount
of his purchase-money to the officer conducting the sale, and in default of such
deposit the property shall forthwith be put up again and re- sold.
