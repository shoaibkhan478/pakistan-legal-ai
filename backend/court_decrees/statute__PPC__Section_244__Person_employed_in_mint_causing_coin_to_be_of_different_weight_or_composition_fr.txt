PPC Section 244: Person employed in mint causing coin to be of different weight or composition from that

fixed by law. Whoever, being employed in any mint lawfully established in
3
[Pakistan], does any act,
or omits what he is legally bound to do, with the intention of causing any coin issued form that mint to
be of a different weight or composition from the weight or composition fixed by law, shall be punished
with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be
liable to fine.
245. Unlawfully  taking  coining  instrument  from  mint. Whoever,  without  lawful  authority,
takes  out  of  any mint, lawfully established in
3
[Pakistan],  any  coining  tool  or  instrument,  shall  be
punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall
also be liable to fine.
