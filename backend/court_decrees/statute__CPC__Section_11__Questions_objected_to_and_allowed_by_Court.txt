CPC Section 11: Questions objected to and allowed by Court

_
Where any question put to
a witness is objected to by a party or his pleader, and the Court allows the same
to be put, the Judge shall take down the question, the answer, the objection and
the name of the person making it, together with the decision of the Court thereon.
