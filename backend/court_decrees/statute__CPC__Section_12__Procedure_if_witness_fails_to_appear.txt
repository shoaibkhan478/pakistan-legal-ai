CPC Section 12: Procedure if witness fails to appear

_
The Court may, where such
person does not appear, or appears but fails so to satisfy the Court, impose
upon him such fine not exceeding
1
[two thousand] rupees as it thinks fit, having
regard to his condition in life and all the circumstances of the case, and may
order his property, or any part thereof, to be attached and sold or, if already
attached under rule 10, to be sold for the purpose of satisfying all costs of such
attachment, together with the amount of the said fine, if any:
Provided that, if the person whose attendance is required pays into
Court the costs and fine aforesaid, the Court shall order the property to be
released from attachment.
