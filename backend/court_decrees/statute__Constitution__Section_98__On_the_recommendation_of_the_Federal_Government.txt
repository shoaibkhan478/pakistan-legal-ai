(2) The net profits earned by the Federal Government, or any
undertaking   established   or   administered   by   the   Federal
Government from the bulk generation of power at a hydro-electric
station shall be paid to the Province in which the hydro-electric sta-
tion is situated.
Explanation.—For the purposes of this clause “net profits”
shall be computed by deducting from the revenues accruing from
the bulk supply of power from the bus-bars of a hydro-electric sta-
tion at a rate to be determined by the Council of Common Interests,
the operating expenses of the station, which shall include any sums
payable as taxes, duties, interest or return on investment, and
depreciations and element of obsolescence, and over-heads, and
provision for reserves.
