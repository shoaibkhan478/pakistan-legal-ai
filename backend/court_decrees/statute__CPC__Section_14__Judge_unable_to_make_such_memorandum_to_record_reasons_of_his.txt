CPC Section 14: Judge unable to make such memorandum to record reasons of his

inability.
_
(1) Where the Judge is unable to make a memorandum as required by
this Order, he shall cause the reason of such inability to be recorded, and shall
cause the memorandum to be made in writing from his dictation in open Court.
(2) Every memorandum so made shall form part of the record.
