CPC Section 42: Attachment in case of decree for rent or mesne profits or other

matter, amount of which to be subsequently determined.
_
Where a decree
directs an inquiry as to rent or mesne profits or any other matter, the property of
the judgment-debtor may, before the amount due from him has been
ascertained, be attached, as in the case of an ordinary decree for the payment of
money.
