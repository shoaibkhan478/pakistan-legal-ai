CPC Section 36: Decree for delivery of immoveable property when in occupancy of

tenant.
_
Where a decree is for the delivery of any immoveable property in the
occupancy of a tenant or other person entitled to occupy the same and not
bound by the decree to relinquish such occupancy, the Court shall order
delivery to be made by affixing a copy of the warrant in some conspicuous place
on the property, and proclaiming to the occupant by beat of drum or other
customary mode, at some convenient place, the substance of the decree in regard
to the property.
