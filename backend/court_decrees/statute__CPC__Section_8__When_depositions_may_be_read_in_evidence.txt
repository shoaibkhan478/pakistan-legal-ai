CPC Section 8: When depositions may be read in evidence

_
Evidence taken under a
commission shall not be read as evidence in the suit without the consent of
the party against whom the same is offered, unless
_
(a) the person who gave the evidence is beyond the jurisdiction of the
Court, or dead or unable from sickness or infirmity to attend to be
personally examined, or exempted from personal appearance in
Court, or is a person in the service of the
1
[State] who cannot, in the
opinion of the Court, attend without detriment to the public service, or
(b) the Court in its discretion dispenses with the proof of any of the
circumstances mentioned in clause (a), and authorises the evidence
of any person being read as evidence in the suit, notwithstanding
proof that the cause for taking such evidence by commission has
ceased at the time of reading the same.
