CPC Section 16: Power to examine witness immediately

_
(1) Where a witness is
about to leave the jurisdiction of the Court, or other sufficient cause is shown
to the satisfaction of the Court why his evidence should be taken immediately,
the Court may upon the application of any party or of the witness, at any time
after the institution of the suit, take the evidence of such witness in manner
hereinbefore provided.
(2) Where such evidence is not taken forthwith and in the presence of
the parties, such notice as the Court thinks sufficient, of the day fixed for the
examination, shall be given to the parties.
(3) The evidence so taken shall be read over to the witness, and, if he
admits it to be correct, shall be signed by him, and the Judge shall, if necessary,
correct the same, and shall sign it, and it may then be read at any hearing of the
suit.
