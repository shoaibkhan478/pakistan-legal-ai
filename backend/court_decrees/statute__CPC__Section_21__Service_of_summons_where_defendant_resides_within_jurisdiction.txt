CPC Section 21: Service of summons where defendant resides within jurisdiction

of another Court.
_
A summons may be sent by the Court by which it is issued,
whether within or without the province, either by one of its officers or by post to any
Court  (not being the High Court) having jurisdiction in the place where the
defendant resides.
