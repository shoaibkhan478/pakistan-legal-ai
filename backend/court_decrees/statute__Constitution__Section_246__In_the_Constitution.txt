251.(1)    The National language of Pakistan is Urdu, and
arrangements shall be made for its being used for official and
other purposes within fifteen years from the commencing day.
(2)    Subject to clause (1), the English language may be
used for official purposes until arrangements are made for its
replacement by Urdu.
(3)    Without prejudice to the status of the National
language, a Provincial Assembly may by law prescribe measures
for the teaching, promotion and use of a provincial language in
addition to the national language.
