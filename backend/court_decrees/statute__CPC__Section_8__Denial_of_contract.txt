CPC Section 8: Denial of contract

_
Where a contract is alleged in any pleading, a bare
denial of the same by the opposite party shall be construed only as a denial in
fact of the express contract alleged or of the matters of fact from which the
same may be implied, and not as a denial of the legality or sufficiency in law of such
contract.
