Constitution Section 31: Corporations, that is to say, the incorporation, regulation

and winding-up of trading corporations, including banking,
insurance   and   financial   corporations,   but   not   including
corporations owned or controlled by a Province and carrying on
business only within that Province, or cooperative societies, and
of corporations, whether trading or not, with objects not confined
to a Province, but not including universities.
