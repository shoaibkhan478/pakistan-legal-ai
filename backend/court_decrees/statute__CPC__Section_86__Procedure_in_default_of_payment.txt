CPC Section 86: Procedure in default of payment

_
In default of payment within the
period mentioned in the last preceding rule, the deposit may, if the Court thinks fit,
after defraying the expenses of the sale, be forfeited to the Government, and the
property shall be re-sold, and the defaulting purchaser shall forfeit all claim to the
property or to any part of the sum for which it may subsequently be sold.
