PPC Section 194: Giving  or  fabricating  false  evidence  with  intent  to  procure  conviction  of      capital

offence; Whoever gives or fabricates false evidence, intending thereby to cause, or knowing it to be likely that
he will thereby cause, any person to be convicted of an offence which is capital
1
[by any law for the time being
in force], shall be punished with
2
[imprisonment for life], or with rigorous imprisonment for a term which may
extend to ten years, and shall also be liable to fine ;
if innocent person be thereby convicted and executed. and if an innocent person be convicted and
executed in consequence of such false evidence, the person who gives such false evidence shall be punished
either with death or the punishment hereinbefore described.
