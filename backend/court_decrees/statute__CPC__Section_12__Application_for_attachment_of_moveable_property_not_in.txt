CPC Section 12: Application for attachment of moveable property not in

judgment-debtor's possession.
_
Where an application is made for the
attachment of any movable property belonging to a judgment-debtor but not in his
possession, the decree-holder shall annex to the application an inventory of the
property to be attached, containing a reasonably accurate description of the same.
