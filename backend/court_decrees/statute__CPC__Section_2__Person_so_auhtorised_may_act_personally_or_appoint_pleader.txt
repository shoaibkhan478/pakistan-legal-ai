CPC Section 2: Person so auhtorised may act personally or appoint pleader

_
Any person authorised by an officer,
3
[soldier, sailor or airman] to prosecute or
defend a suit in his stead may prosecute or defend it in person in the same
manner as the officer,
4
[soldier, sailor or airman] could do if present; or he may
appoint a pleader to prosecute or defend the suit on behalf of such officer,
5
[soldier,
sailor or airman].
