1

under Act of the Provincial Assembly of any other Province.
(2)    If a trade or business of any kind is carried on by or on
behalf of the Government of a Province outside that Province, that
Government may, in respect of any property used in connection
with that trade or business or any income arising from that trade or
business, be taxed under Act of
1
[Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament)] or
under Act of the Provincial Assembly of the Province in which that
trade or business is carried on.
(3)    Nothing in this Article shall prevent the imposition of
fees for services rendered.
