CPC Section 1: Modes of paying money under decree

_
(1) All money payable under a
decree shall be paid as follows, namely:-
a) into the Court whose duty it is to execute the decree; or
b) out of Court to the decree-holder
1
[through a bank or by postal
money order or evidence by writing signed by the decree-
holder or his authorized agent]; or
c) otherwise as the Court which made the decree directs.
(2) Where any payment is made under clause (a) of sub-rule (1) notice
of such payment shall be given to the decree-holder.
