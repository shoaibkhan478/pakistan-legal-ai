CPC Section 1: Notice to the Advocate General

_
In any suit in which it appears to
the Court that
7
[any substantial question as to the interpretation of
constitutional law] is involved, the Court shall not proceed to determine that
question until after notice has been given to the
8
[Attorney General for Pakistan] if
the question of law concerns the
9
[Federal Government] and to the
Advocate-General of the Province if the question of law concerns a Provincial
Government.
