CPC Section 14: Pleading to be signed

_
Every pleading shall be signed by the party and
his pleader (if any):
Provided that where a party pleading is, by reason of absence or for other
good cause, unable to sign the pleading, it may be signed by any person duly
authorized by him to sign the same or to sue or defend on his behalf.
