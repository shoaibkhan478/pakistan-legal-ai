Constitution Section 6: A candidate may withdraw his candidature at any time

before noon on the day fixed for this purpose by delivering
a notice in writing under his hand to the Presiding Officer
with whom his nomination paper has been deposited, and a
candidate who has given a notice of withdrawal of his
candidature under this paragraph shall not be allowed to
cancel that notice.
