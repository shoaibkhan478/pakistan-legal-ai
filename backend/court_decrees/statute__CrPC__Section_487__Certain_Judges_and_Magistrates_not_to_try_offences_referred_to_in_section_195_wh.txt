CHAPTER XXXVII
DIRECTIONS OF THE NATURE OF A Habeas Corpus
491. Power  to  issue  directions  of  the  nature  of  a  habeas  carpus.-(1)
7
[Any  High  Court]
may, whenever it thinks fit, direct-
(a) that a person within the limits of its
8

Court to be dealt with according to law;
(b) that a person illegally or improperly detained in public or private  custody within  such limits  be
set at liberty ;
(c) that a prisoner detained in any jail situate within such limits be brought before the Court to be
there examined as a witness in any matter pending or to be inquired into in such Court;
(d) that a prisoner detained as aforesaid be brought before a Court-martial or any Commissioners
9

* * for trial or to be examined touching any matter pending before such Court-martial or
Commissioners respectively ;
(e) that a prisoner within such limits be removed from one custody to another for the  purpose  of
trial; and
(f) that the body of a defendant within such limits be brought in on the  Sheriff's return of Cepi
Corpus to a writ of attachment.
