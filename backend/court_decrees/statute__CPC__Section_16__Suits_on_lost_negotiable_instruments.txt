CPC Section 16: Suits on lost negotiable instruments

_
Where the suit is founded upon a
negotiable instrument, and it is proved that the instrument is lost, and an indemnity
is given by the plaintiff, to the satisfaction of the Court, against the claims of any
other person upon such instrument, the Court may pass such decree as it would
have passed if the plaintiff had produced the instrument in Court when the plaint
was presented, and had at the same time delivered a copy of the instrument to be
filed with the plaint.
