Constitution Section 4: At any time before noon on the day fixed for nomina

tion any member of the Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament) or of
a Provincial Assembly may nominate for election as
President a person qualified for election as President by
delivering to the Presiding Officer a nomination paper,
signed by himself as proposer and by another member of
the Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament) or, as the case may be
Assembly as seconder, together with a statement signed by
the person nominated that he cons ents to the nomination:
Provided that no person shall subscribe, whether as
proposer or as seconder, more than one nomination paper at
any one election.
