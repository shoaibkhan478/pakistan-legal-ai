CPC Section 5: Service of process on pleader

_
Any process served on the pleader of
any party or left at the office or ordinary residence of such pleader, and whether
the same is for the personal appearance of the party or not, shall be presumed
to be duly communicated and made known to the party whom the pleader
represents, and, unless the Court otherwise directs, shall be as effectual for all
purposes as if the same had been given to or served on the party in person.
