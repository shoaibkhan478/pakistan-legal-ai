CPC Section 5: And it is hereby further ordered and decreed that the money realised by

such sale shall be paid into Court and be duly applied (after deduction therefrom
of the expenses of the sale) first in payment of the amount due to the plaintiff as
specified in clause 1 above with such costs of the suit and other costs, charges
and expenses as may be payable under rule 10, together with such subsequent
interest as may be payable under rule 11, of Order XXXIV of the First Schedule to
the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, and that the balance, if any, shall be applied
in payment of the amount due to defendant No.2; and that, if any balance be left,
it shall be paid to defendant No.1 or other persons entitled to receive the same.
