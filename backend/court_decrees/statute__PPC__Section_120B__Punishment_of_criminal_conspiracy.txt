PPC Section 120B: Punishment of criminal conspiracy

(1) Whoever is a party to a criminal conspiracy to
commit an offence punishable with death,
7
[imprisonment for life] or rigorous imprisonment for a  term
of two years or upwards, shall, where no express provision is made in this Code for the punishment of
such a conspiracy, be punished in the same manner as if he had abetted such offence.
(2) Whoever is a  party  to  a criminal conspiracy  other  than  a criminal conspiracy  to commit an
offence punishable as aforesaid shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term
not exceeding six months, or with fine or with both.]
