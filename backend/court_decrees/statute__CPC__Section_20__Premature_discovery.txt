CPC Section 20: Premature discovery

_
Where the party from whom discovery of any
kind or inspection is sought objects to the same, or any part thereof, the
Court may, if satisfied that the right to the discovery or inspection sought
depends on the determination of any issue or question in dispute in the suit, or
that for any other reason it is desirable that any issue or question in dispute in the
suit should be determined before deciding upon the right to the discovery or
inspection, order that such issue or question be determined first, and reserve the
question as to the discovery or inspection.
