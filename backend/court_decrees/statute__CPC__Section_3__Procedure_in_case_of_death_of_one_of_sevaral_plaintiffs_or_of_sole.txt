CPC Section 3: Procedure in case of death of one of sevaral plaintiffs or of sole

plaintiff.
_
(1) Where one of two or more plaintiffs dies and the right to sue does
not survive to the surviving plaintiffs alone
1
[, or on receipt of an intimation of the
death of such plaintiff from the person nominated by him for that purpose under
rule 26, Order VII], or a sole plaintiff or sole surviving plaintiff dies and the right to
sue survives, the Court, on an application made in that behalf, shall cause the
legal representative of the deceased plaintiff to be made a party and shall proceed
with the suit.
2
[(2) Where within the time allowed by law no application is made or
intimation is given under sub-rule (1), the Court may proceed with the suit, and
any order made or judgment pronounced in such suit shall, notwithstanding the
death of such plaintiff, have the same force and effect as if it had been made or
pronounced before the death took place.]
