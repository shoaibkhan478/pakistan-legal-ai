CPC Section 13: Application for attachment of immoveable property to contain

certain particulars.
_
Where an application is made for the attachment of any
immoveable property belonging to a judgment-debtor, it shall contain at the foot-
(a) a description of such property sufficient to identify the same
and, in case such property can be identified by boundaries or
numbers in a record of settlement or survey, a specification of
such boundaries or numbers; and
(b) a specification of the judgment-debtor's share or interest in
such property to the best of the belief of the applicant, and so
far as he has been able to ascertain the same.
