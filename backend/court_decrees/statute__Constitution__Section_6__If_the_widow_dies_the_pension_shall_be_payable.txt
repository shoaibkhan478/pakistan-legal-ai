Constitution Section 6: If the widow dies, the pension shall be payable–

(a) to the sons of the Judge who are less than twenty-one
years of age, until they attain that age; and
(b) to the unmarried daughters of the Judge who are less
than twenty-one years of age, until they attain that age
or are married, whichever first occurs.]
