CPC Section 13: Where rejection of plaint does not preclude presentation of fresh

plaint.
_
The rejection of the plaint on any of the grounds hereinbefore mentioned
shall not of its own force preclude the plaintiff from presenting a fresh plaint in
respect of the same cause of action.
