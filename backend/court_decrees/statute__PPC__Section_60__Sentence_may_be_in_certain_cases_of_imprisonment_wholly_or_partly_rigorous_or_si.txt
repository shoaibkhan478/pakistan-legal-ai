PPC Section 60: Sentence may be (in certain cases of imprisonment) wholly or partly rigorous or simple

In every case in which an offender is punishable with imprisonment which may be of either description,
it shall  be  competent  to  the  Court  which  sentences  such  offender  to  direct in the  sentence  that  such
imprisonment shall be wholly rigorous, or that such imprisonment shall be wholly simple, or that any
part of such imprisonment shall be rigorous and the rest simple.
