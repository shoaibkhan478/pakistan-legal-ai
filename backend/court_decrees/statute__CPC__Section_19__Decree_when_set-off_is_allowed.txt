CPC Section 19: Decree when set-off is allowed

_
(1) Where the defendant has been
allowed a set-off against the claim of the plaintiff, the decree shall state what
amount is due to the plaintiff and what amount is due to the defendant, and shall
be for the recovery of any sum which appears to be due to either party.
(2) Appeal from decree relating to set-off.
_
Any decree passed in
a suit in which a set-off is claimed shall be subject to the same provisions in
respect of appeal to which it would have been subject if no set-off had been
claimed.
(3) The provisions of this rule shall apply whether the set-off is
admissible under rule 6 of Order VIII or otherwise.
