CPC Section 21: Non-compliance with order for discovery

_
Where any party fails to
comply with any order to answer interrogatories, or for discovery or inspection of
documents, he shall, if a plaintiff, be liable to have his suit dismissed for want of
prosecution, and, if a defendant, to have his defence, if any, struck out, and to be
placed in the same position as if he had not defended, and the party
interrogating or seeking discovery or inspection may apply to the Court for an order
to that effect, and an order may be made accordingly.
