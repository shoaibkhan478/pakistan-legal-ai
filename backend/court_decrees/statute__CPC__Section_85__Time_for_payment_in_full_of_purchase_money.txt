CPC Section 85: Time for payment in full of purchase money

_
The full amount of
purchase-money payable shall be paid by the purchaser into Court before the
Court closes on the fifteenth day from the sale of the property:
Provided that, in calculating the amount to be so paid into Court, the
purchaser shall have the advantage of any set-off to which he may be entitled
under rule 72.
