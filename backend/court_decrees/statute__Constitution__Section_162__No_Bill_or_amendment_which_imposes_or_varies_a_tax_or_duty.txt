Constitution Section 162: No Bill or amendment which imposes or varies a tax or duty

the whole or part of the net proceeds whereof is assigned to any
Province, or which varies the meaning of the expression “agricul-
tural income” as defined for the purposes of the enactments relat-
ing to income-tax, or which affects the principles on which under
any of the foregoing provisions of this Chapter moneys are or may
be distributable to Provinces, shall be introduced or moved in the
National Assembly except with the previous sanction of the
President.
