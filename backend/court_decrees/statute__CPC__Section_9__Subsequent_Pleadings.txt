CPC Section 9: Subsequent Pleadings

_
No pleading subsequent to the written statement
of a defendant other than by way of defence to a set-off shall be presented
except by the leave of the Court and upon such terms as the Court thinks fit, but
the Court may at any time require a written statement or additional written
statement from any of the parties and fix a time for presenting the same.
