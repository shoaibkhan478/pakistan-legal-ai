CPC Section 18: Failure to amend after order

_
If a party who has obtained an order for
leave to amend does not amend accordingly within the time limited for that
purpose by the order, or if no time is thereby limited then within fourteen days
from the date of the order, he shall not be permitted to amend after the expiration
of such limited time as aforesaid or of such fourteen days, as the case may be,
unless the time is extended by the Court.
