PPC Section 511: Punishment for attempting to commit offences punishable with

2
[imprisonment for life
or  for  a  shorter  term]. Whoever attempts  to  commit  an  offence punishable  by this  Code  with
3
[imprisonment for life], or imprisonment, or to cause such an offence to be committed, and in such
attempt does any act towards the commission of the offence, shall, where no express provision is
made by this Code for the punishment of such attempt be punished with
4
[imprisonment of any
description provided for the offence, for a term which may extend to one-half of the longest term
of imprisonment provided for that offence] or with such fine
5
[daman] as is provided for the offence,
or with both.
Illustrations
(a) A makes an attempt to steal some jewels by breaking open a box, and finds after so opening the box, that
there is no jewel in it. He has done an act towards the commission of theft, and therefore is guilty under
this section.
(b) A makes an attempt to pick the pocket of Z by thrusting his hand into Z’s pocket. A fails in the attempt in
consequence of Z’s having nothing in his pocket. A is guilty under this section.
