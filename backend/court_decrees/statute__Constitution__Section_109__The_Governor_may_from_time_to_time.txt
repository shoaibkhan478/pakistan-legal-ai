Constitution Section 109: The Governor may from time to time–

(a)    summon the Provincial Assembly to meet at such time
and place as he thinks fit; and
(b)    prorogue the Provincial Assembly.
110.The Governor may address the Provincial Assembly and
may for that purpose require the attendance of the members.
