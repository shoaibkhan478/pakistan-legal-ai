CPC Section 6: Objections to interrogatories by answer

_
Any objection to answering
any interrogatory on the ground that it is scandalous or irrelevant or not exhibited
bona fide for the purpose of the suit, or that the matters inquired into are not
sufficiently material at that stage, or on any other ground, may be taken in the
affidavit in answer.
