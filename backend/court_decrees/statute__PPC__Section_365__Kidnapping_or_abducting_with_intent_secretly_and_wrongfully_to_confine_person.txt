PPC Section 365: Kidnapping  or  abducting  with  intent  secretly  and  wrongfully  to  confine  person

Whoever kidnaps or abducts any person with intent to cause that person to be secretly and wrongfully
confined, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to
seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.

Whoever
kidnaps or abducts any woman with intent that she may be compelled, or knowing it to be likely that

punished as aforesaid.]
