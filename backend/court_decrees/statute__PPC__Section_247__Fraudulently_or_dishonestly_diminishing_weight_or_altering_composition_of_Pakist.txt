PPC Section 247: Fraudulently  or  dishonestly  diminishing  weight  or  altering  composition  of  Pakistan

coin. Whoever  fraudulently  or  dishonestly  performs  on
1
[any  Pakistan  coin],  any  operation  which
diminishes the weight or alters the composition of that coin, shall be punished with imprisonment of
either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.
