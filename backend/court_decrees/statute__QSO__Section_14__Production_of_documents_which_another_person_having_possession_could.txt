QSO Section 14: Production  of  documents  which  another  person,  having  possession,  could

refuse to produce. — No one shall be compelled to produce documents in his possession, which any
other  person  would  be  entitled  to  refuse  to  produce  if  they  were  in  his  possession,  unless  such  last-
mentioned person consents to their production.
