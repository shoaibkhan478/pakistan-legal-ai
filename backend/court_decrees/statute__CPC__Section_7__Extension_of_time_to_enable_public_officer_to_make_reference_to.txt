CPC Section 7: Extension of time to enable public officer to make reference to

Government.
_
(1) Where the defendant is a public officer and, on receiving
the summons, considers it proper to make a reference to
1
[the Government]
before answering the plaint, he may apply to the Court to grant such extension of
the time fixed in the summons as may be necessary to enable him to make such
reference and to receive orders thereon through the proper channel.
(2) Upon such application the Court shall extend the time for so long
as appears to it to be necessary.
