CPC Section 4: Only certain claims to be joined for recovery of immoveable

property.
_
No cause of action shall, unless with the leave of the Court, be joined
with a suit for the recovery of immoveable property, except
_
(a) claims for mesne profits or arrears of rent in respect of the property
claimed or any part thereof;
(b) claims for damages for breach of any contract under which the
property or any part thereof is held; and
(c) claims in which the relief sought is based on the same cause of
action:
Provided that nothing in this rule shall be deemed to prevent any party in a
suit for foreclosure or redemption from asking to be put into possession of the
mortgaged property.
