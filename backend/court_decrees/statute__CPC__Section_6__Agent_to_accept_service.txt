CPC Section 6: Agent to accept service

_
(1) Besides the recognized agents described
in rule 2 any person residing within the jurisdiction of the Court may be appointed
an agent to accept service of process.
(2) Appointment to be in writing and to be filed in Court.
_
Such
appointment may be special or general and shall be made by an instrument in
writing signed by the principal, and such instrument or, if the appointment is general,
a certified copy thereof shall be filed in Court.
