Constitution Section 16: A ballot paper shall be invalid if–

(i)    there is upon it any name, word or mark, by
which the person voting may be identified; or
(ii)    it does not contain the initials of the Presiding
Officer; or
(iii)    it does not contain mark; or
(iv)    a mark is placed against the names of two or more
candidates ; or
(v)    there is any uncertainty as to the identity of the
candidate against whose name the mark is placed.
