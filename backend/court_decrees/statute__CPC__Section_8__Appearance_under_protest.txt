CPC Section 8: Appearance under protest

_
Any person served with summons as a
partner under rule 3 may appear under protest, denying that he is a partner, but
such appearance shall not preclude the plaintiff from otherwise serving a
summons on the firm and obtaining a decree against the firm in default of
appearance where no partner has appeared.
