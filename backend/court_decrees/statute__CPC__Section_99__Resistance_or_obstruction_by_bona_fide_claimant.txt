CPC Section 99: Resistance or obstruction by bona fide claimant

_
Where the Court
is satisfied that the resistance or obstruction was occasioned by any person
(other than the judgment-debtor) claiming in good faith to be in possession
of the property on his own account or on account of some person other than the
judgment-debtor, the Court shall make an order dismissing the application.
