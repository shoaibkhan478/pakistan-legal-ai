PPC Section 457: Lurking  house  trespass  or  house-breaking  by  night  in  order  to  commit  offence

punishable with imprisonment. Whoever commits lurking house-trespass by night, or house- breaking
by night, in order to the committing of any offence  punishable with imprisonment, shall be punished
with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to five years, and shall also be
liable to fine ; and, if the offence intended to be committed is theft, the term of the imprisonment may
be extended to fourteen years.
