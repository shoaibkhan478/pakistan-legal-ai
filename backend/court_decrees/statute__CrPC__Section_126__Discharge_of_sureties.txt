CrPC Section 126: Discharge of sureties

(1) Any surety for the peaceable conduct or good behaviour of
another person may at any time apply to a
5
* *  *,[concerned Magistrate  of the  first class]  to
cancel any bond executed under this Chapter within the local limits of his jurisdiction.
(2) On such application being made, the Magistrate shall issue his summons or warrant, as he thinks
fit,  requiring the person for  whom such surety is bound to appear or to be brought before  him.

[126A.]  Security  for  unexpired  period  of  bond.

126, sub-section (2), appears or is brought before him, the Magistrate shall cancel the bond executed
by such person] and shall order such person to give, for  the  unexpired portion of the  term of such
bond, fresh  security of the  same description  as the original security. Every such order shall, for  the
purposes  of  sections  121,  122,  123  and  124,  be  deemed  to  be  an  order  made  under  section    106  or
section 118, as the case may be.
