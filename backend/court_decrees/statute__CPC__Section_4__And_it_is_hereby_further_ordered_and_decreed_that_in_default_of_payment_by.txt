CPC Section 4: And it is hereby further ordered and decreed that, in default of payment by

defendants Nos. 1 and 2 as aforesaid, the plaintiff may apply to the court for
a final decree for sale, and on such application being made the mortgaged
property or a sufficient part thereof shall be directed to be sold; and that for the
purposes of such sale the plaintiff and defendant No.2 shall produce before the
Court or such officer as it appoints, all documents in their possession or power
relating to the mortgaged property.
