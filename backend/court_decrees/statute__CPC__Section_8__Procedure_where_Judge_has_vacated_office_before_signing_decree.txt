CPC Section 8: Procedure where Judge has vacated office before signing decree

_
Where a Judge has vacated office after pronouncing judgment but without signing
the decree, a decree drawn up in accordance with such judgment may be signed
by his successor or, if the Court has ceased to exist, by the Judge of any Court to
which such Court was subordinate.
