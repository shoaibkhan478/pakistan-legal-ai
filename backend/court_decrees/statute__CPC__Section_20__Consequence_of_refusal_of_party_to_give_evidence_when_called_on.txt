CPC Section 20: Consequence of refusal of party to give evidence when called on

by Court.
_
Where any party to a suit present in Court refuses, without lawful
excuse, when required by the Court, to give evidence or to produce any
document then and there in his possession or power, the Court may
pronounce judgment against him or make such order in relation to the suit as it
thinks fit.
