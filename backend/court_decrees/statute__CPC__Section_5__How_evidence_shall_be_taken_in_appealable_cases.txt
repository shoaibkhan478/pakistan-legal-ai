CPC Section 5: How evidence shall be taken in appealable cases

_
In cases in which an
appeal is allowed the evidence of each witness shall be taken down in writing, in
the language of the Court, by or in the presence and under the personal
direction and superintendence of the Judge, not ordinarily in the form of question
and answer, but in that of a narrative, and, when completed, shall be read
over in the presence of the Judge and of the witness, and the Judge shall, if
necessary, correct the same, and shall sign it.
