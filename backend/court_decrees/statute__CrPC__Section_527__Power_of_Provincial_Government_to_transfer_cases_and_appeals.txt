CrPC Section 527: Power      of      Provincial      Government      to      transfer      cases      and      appeals

_
(1)   The
5
[Provincial Government] may, by notification in the
6
[Official Gazette], direct the transfer of any particular
7
* case or appeal from one High Court to another High Court, or from any Criminal Court subordinate to one
High  Court, to any other Criminal Court of equal or superior jurisdiction subordinate to  another High Court,
whenever it appears to
8
[it] that such transfer will promote the ends of justice, or tend to the general convenience
of parties or witnesses :
9
[Provided  that  no  case  or  appeal  shall  be  transferred  to  a  High  Court  or  other  Court  in  another
Province without the consent of the Provincial Government of that Province.]
(2) The Court to which such case or appeal is transferred shall deal with the same as if it had been originally
instituted in, or presented to, such Court.
