CPC Section 6: Endorsements on documents rejected as inadmissible in

evidence.
_
Where a document relied on as evidence by either party is
considered by the Court to be inadmissible in evidence, there shall be endorsed
thereon the particulars mentioned in clauses  (a), (b) and (c) of rule 4, sub-rule
(1) together with a statement of its having been rejected, and the endorsement
shall be signed or initialled by the Judge.
