CPC Section 9: Suits between co-partners

_
This Order shall apply to suits between a
firm and one or more of the partners therein and to suits between firms having
one or more partners in common; but no execution shall be issued in such suits
except by leave of the Court, and, on an application for leave to issue such
execution, all such accounts and inquiries may be directed to be taken and
made and directions given as may be just.
