CPC Section 78: Irregularity not to vitiate sale, but any person injured may

sue.
_
No irregularity in  publishing or conducting the sale of moveable property
shall vitiate the sale; but any person sustaining any injury by reason of such
irregularity at the hand of any other person may institute a suit against him for
compensation or  (if such other person is the purchaser) for the recovery of
the specific property and for compensation in default of such recovery.
