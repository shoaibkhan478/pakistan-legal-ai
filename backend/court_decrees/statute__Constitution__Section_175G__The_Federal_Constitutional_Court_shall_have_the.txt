Constitution Section 175G: The Federal Constitutional Court shall have the

power, subject to the provisions of any Act of Majlis-e-Shoora
(Parliament) and any rules made by the Federal Constitutional
Court, to review any judgment pronounced or any order made by
it.
175H.        (1) If, at any time, the President considers that it is
desirable to obtain the opinion of the Federal Constitutional
Court on any question of law which he considers of public
importance,   he   may   refer   the   question   to   the   Federal
Constitutional Court for consideration.
(2)   The Federal Constitutional Court shall consider a
question so referred and report its opinion on the question to the
President.
