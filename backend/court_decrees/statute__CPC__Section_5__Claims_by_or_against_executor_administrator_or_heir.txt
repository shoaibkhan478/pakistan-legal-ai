CPC Section 5: Claims by or against executor, administrator or heir

_
No claim by
or against an executor, administrator or heir, as such, shall be joined with claims
by or against him personally, unless the last mentioned claims are alleged to arise
with reference to the estate in respect of which the plaintiff or defendant sues or
is sued as executor, administrator or heir, or are such as he was entitled to, or
liable for, jointly with the deceased person whom he represents.
