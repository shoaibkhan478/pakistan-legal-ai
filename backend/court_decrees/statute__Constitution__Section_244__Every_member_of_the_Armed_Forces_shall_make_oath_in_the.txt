Constitution Section 244: Every member of the Armed Forces shall make oath in the

form set out in the Third Schedule.
245.
1
[(1)] The Armed Forces shall, under the directions of the
Federal Government defend Pakistan against external aggression
or threat of war, and, subject to law, act in aid of civil power
when called upon to do so.
2
[(2) The validity of any direction issued by the Federal
Government under clause (1) shall not be called in question in
any court.
(3)    A High Court shall not exercise any jurisdiction under
Article 199 in relation to any area in which the Armed Forces of
Pakistan are, for the time being, acting in aid of civil power in
pursuance of Article 245:
Provided that this clause shall not be deemed to affect the
jurisdiction of the High Court in respect of any proceeding
pending immediately before the day on which the Armed Forces
start acting in aid of civil power.
